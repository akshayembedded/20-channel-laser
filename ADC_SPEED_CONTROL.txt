ADC SPEED CONTROL (current 10x10 laser firmware)
==============================================
main.c now reads a potentiometer on PE6 / AIN9, LQFP48 pin 24.
Connect a 10 kohm linear potentiometer between VDDA and VSSA (ground),
with its wiper connected to PE6. Keep input voltage within VSSA..VDDA.
An optional 100 nF capacitor from the wiper to VSSA reduces noise.
Low voltage gives fast patterns; high voltage gives slow patterns.
Swap the two outside pot wires to reverse knob direction.

PE6 controls all 22 animated patterns. The steady all-on boot is static.
TIM4 IRQ23 starts an ADC conversion about every 10 ms at the reset 2 MHz clock.
ADC1 IRQ22 stores the latest 10-bit result. Speed defaults to midscale until
the first sample. Both animation boots initialize ADC and TIM4 interrupts.

A frame weight of 220 maps to roughly 100 ms..1 second per step.
HTML frame weights scale that range proportionally, rounded to TIM4 ticks.
Orbit relay and the reverse single-point chase use weight 80 (roughly 40..360 ms).
Both full-circle chases use weight 220 and direct row drive.
Strobing uses weight 320 and switches columns with all rows enabled.
Row relays stay selected while the POT controls how long the columns display
each step. A dark step blanks columns and keeps the row selection.
Rows with the same column mask share a step. Different masks play as separate
groups, each with its own POT-controlled hold, so these frames take longer.
Before a row selection changes, the firmware blanks the columns. It waits
RELAY_SETTLE_TICKS after releasing rows and again after picking up rows.
The default is 5 ticks (about 50 ms per phase). Set it to suit the fitted relays.

The exact timing and brightness depend on the board. Build with Cosmic/STVD.
Host checks: node tools/verify_circle_timing.js
See MODE_COMPARISON.txt for the 18 HTML presets and their firmware equivalents.
